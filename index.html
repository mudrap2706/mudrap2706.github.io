Data Management & Engineering

Toronto Bicycle Thefts: End-to-End Data Pipeline & BI Dashboard

GitHubFinal Report

Challenge — Ten years of open municipal crime data (39,000+ records) needed to move from raw CSV to a live, explorable analytics layer usable by non-technical stakeholders.

My role — Led data modeling and SQL analysis on a 3-person team: designed the MySQL schema, wrote 15 analytical queries spanning temporal, geographic, attribute, and multi-dimensional analysis, and architected 6 pre-aggregated views plus a master table optimized for the BI layer. Teammates handled cloud infrastructure/ETL and the QuickSight dashboard build.

Fig. 1. Full dashboard: KPI cockpit, geographic map, trend line, weekly risk matrix, and brand targeting, unified through a single master table for cross-filtering.

Outcomes

  • Schema and query design supporting sub-second dashboard performance across 39,000+ records
  • Recovery rates under 2% in every police division; 53.85% of thefts concentrated at outdoor/street and apartment locations
  • Master table architecture enabled global filtering without precomputing every possible aggregation
